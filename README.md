# Hi, I'm Marcos (Ubermoc)

I've been into tech since I was 12, and I've played World of Warcraft with a controller for 15 years. I make free tools so you can play from the couch with just a controller, on the Steam Deck or any Linux gaming PC.

## What I make

- **[Ubermoc UI](https://github.com/ubermoc/Ubermoc-UI)** ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/ubermoc-ui)): a controller-first World of Warcraft interface. Play without a mouse or keyboard.
- **[Ubermoc Speech](https://github.com/ubermoc/Ubermoc-Speech)**: a Decky Loader plugin. Hold a button and talk instead of typing.
- **[Ubermoc Warcraft Mod Updater](https://github.com/ubermoc/Ubermoc-Warcraft-Mod-Updater)**: a Decky Loader plugin that installs and updates your WoW addons from Gaming Mode.

Together: the Updater keeps the addon current, Ubermoc UI lets you play, and Ubermoc Speech does the typing.
